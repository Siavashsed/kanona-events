Kanona Projects  ·  Founded 2024

The people
behind the atmosphere.

An ongoing series of experiments in sensory design, intimate gatherings, and the deliberate architecture of evenings that stay with you.

Experiential Curator  ·  Creative Director
Kani Sedighi

I did not start by designing events. I started by noticing what made certain evenings impossible to forget and others impossible to remember - and realizing the difference was almost never the food or the venue.

It was the scent when you walked in. The specific temperature of the light. The silence that settled between the second and third course. The fact that the ten people in the room had something real to offer each other.

Kanona Projects is my attempt to make those things intentional. To treat atmosphere the way a composer treats a score - as structure, not decoration.

The writing on this site is part of the practice. An attempt to articulate what we are building before we build it, and what we learned after we did.

"An event is not a gathering of people. It is a gathering of moments - and the architect's job is to make sure each one is worth keeping."

The Approach
01
Sensory First

Every Kanona event begins with the olfactory layer. We choose the scent before we choose the menu. The fragrance at the door is the first sentence - and first sentences are not written last.

02
Deliberate Silence

We design for the pauses. The moment before the music begins. The quiet between courses. Most event planners fill these spaces. We protect them.

03
Curated Presence

We have never filled a seat simply because it was available. Who is in the room determines what happens in the room. The guest list is written like a story - with tension, contrast, and resolution.

04
No Templates

The specific combination of people, weather, light, and conversation that made a Kanona evening what it was cannot be reproduced. We design for the one occasion. Nothing is repeated.
